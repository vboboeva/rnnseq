# Relating weight-matrix SVs to activity PCs (last timestep) and to transition labels

## Context

Observation to explain: projecting last-timestep activity onto SV1 of the recurrent weight
matrix separates classes by the **latest** transition (same AA vs different AB), SV2 by the
**previous** transition; PC1/PC2 of the same activity separate the 7 clusters less well, and
a third PC is needed. Ask: a *simple* analysis of how the weight SVs relate to the activity
PCs. Same data and sim set as the U·V overlap work (`outBiasTrue`, N=160, 100 sims,
7 classes, `cue_size=4`, `L=0`, `delay=0`, so T=4 timesteps = the 4 cue letters).

No existing script does this. Searched the main tree, the uv-proj worktree and the siblings
(`RNN_manysims`, `RNN_rankconstrained`, `RNN_lowrank_avg`, `RNN_contdata_lowrank`,
`RNNClass_PCA`, `RNN_backup5Feb2025`). What exists, and what is missing:

- `lowrank.py` (all copies) projects activity on `Vh` (`all_hs_proj`, `lowrank.py:159-162`)
  but only as heatmaps; it never scatters by transition and never forms `Vh_W @ Vh_PC.T`.
  Its "activity PCs" are an **uncentred** `np.linalg.svd(h)` (`lowrank.py:123`).
- `RNN_manysims/plot_pca.py` (also `RNNClass_PCA/plot_pca.py`) has the centred sklearn PCA
  (`embedding`, l.~385) and a 3-plane scatter (`plot_3D_PCA_2D_proj`, l.233). It depends on
  `make_data`, hard-codes `small_list=['abbb',...]`, and its `'transitions'` colouring is
  broken (undefined `tokens`) and uses the *letter pair*, not same/different.
- No same/different label exists anywhere; `plot_alignment_activity_trans` means "time t to
  t+1", not a letter transition. The labels must be derived (below).
- Missing everywhere: SV-vs-PC overlap matrix, variance of activity along each SV, label
  decoding per axis.

### Labels (derived from the token string, not the class index)

Class = template over {a,b}: `abbb abba abab abaa aabb aaba aaab`. Define
`t_k = (token[k] != token[k+1])` for k=0,1,2 → **first** (letters 1-2), **previous**
(2-3), **latest** (3-4). The 7 classes are exactly the 7 non-constant combinations of these
three bits, so the three labels are the complete description of the classes and are nearly
orthogonal across classes (pairwise correlation −1/6). Within the four (previous, latest)
groups, SS={abbb}, SD={abba,aaab}, DD={abab,aaba}, DS={abaa,aabb}, the two members differ only
in the **first** transition — which is what a third axis must resolve to separate all 7.
Deriving from letters sidesteps any ambiguity in `token_to_type` indexing.

### Preview (read-only, 4 sims, last epoch/time step, 630 train sequences each)

η² = squared correlation between an axis coordinate and a binary label.

| axis | first | previous | latest |
|---|---|---|---|
| SV1 (`X@Vh[0]`) | 0.01–0.15 | 0.03–0.09 | **0.72–0.87** (4/4 sims) |
| SV2 | ≤0.09 | **0.89–0.92** in 3/4 sims; 0.50 in sim 0 | ≤0.05 |
| SV3 | ≤0.24 | 0.19–0.36 in sims 1–3; **0.90 in sim 0** | ≤0.03 |
| centred PC1 (var 36–45%) | **0.86–0.97** | ≤0.07 | ≤0.04 |
| centred PC2 (var 18–21%) | ≤0.01 | **0.84–0.92** | ≤0.04 |
| centred PC3, PC4 (var 2–6%) | ≈0 | ≈0 | ≈0 |

and `|cos(SV_i, PC_j)|` for i,j ≤3 (chance 1/√160 = 0.079): SV1↔PC1 = 0.02–0.07 (chance
level) and SV1↔PC2,PC3 ≤ 0.12; SV2↔PC1 reaches 0.20 in sims 1 and 3; SV2↔PC2 = 0.79–0.81 in
sims 1–3 (0.26 in sim 0, where SV3↔PC2 = 0.63); a few other entries are 0.2–0.25.

Reading (4 sims only, to be confirmed on all 100): the SV1→latest and SV2→previous claim
holds, with the "previous" axis sometimes on SV3 — the same v2/v3 identity swap seen in the
U·V overlap (59/33). **But PC1 is the *first* transition, not the latest, and has chance-level
overlap with SV1 (≤0.20 with SV2, SV3); the latest transition is not visible in PC1–PC4 at all.** So "PCs ≈
SVs" is not the right picture: SV1 carries a low-variance axis, PC1 a high-variance axis on
a different part of the weight spectrum. The plan therefore *measures where each PC sits in
the SV spectrum* instead of assuming the SV1/SV2↔PC1/PC2 correspondence.

### Stage 1 — per-sim axes
- Weight side: `U,S,Vh = svd(W_rec)`. **Primary = right singular vectors `Vh`** (read-in
  directions; the existing `h @ Vh.T` convention); `U` only as a control in the overlap
  panel.
- Activity side: **centred** PCA of last-timestep activity (sklearn-`embedding` convention;
  an uncentred SVD, as in `lowrank.py`, mostly returns the mean direction). The uncentred
  variant is reported as a control with the mean's share of ‖h‖² printed.
- Coordinates `Z_sv = X_last @ Vh[:K].T`, `Z_pc = Xc @ P[:K].T`, K=10.

### Stage 2 — how the two bases relate (per sim, then summarised)
All gauge-free (sign flips of SV or PC change nothing: only |·|, squares, subspaces).
1. `|cos(SV_i, PC_j)|`, K×K, with the 1/√N chance level marked.
2. **Where each PC lives in the SV spectrum**: for j≤4, mass `(Vh_i·P_j)²` over *all* N
   SVs, and its cumulative sum vs r. Compare with the r/N baseline. This answers "is PC1 on
   SV4+?" directly, and is the analogue of the earlier `rank_profile`.
3. **Principal angles** between span(SV₁..r) and span(PC₁..r), r=1..10 (singular values of
   `Vh[:r] P[:r].T`).
4. **Energy per axis**: centred variance of the activity along SV_k vs along PC_k (fraction
   of total), and cumulative variance captured by top-r SVs vs top-r PCs (the upper bound)
   vs r/N. Tests the "latest transition lives in a low-variance direction" reading.

### Stage 3 — what each axis encodes (the part that shows the user's observation)
`eta2(coord, label)` = squared Pearson r (= between-group variance share for a binary
label), computed for each axis (SV₁..K and PC₁..K) × label (first, previous, latest).
- Per-sim matrices → mean and 5–95% band over sims.
- **Winner axis** per label per sim, `argmax_k eta2`, as a histogram: expect latest→SV1,
  previous→SV2 or SV3 (the identity swap), first→PC1 but *not* an SV among the top few.
- Null: **class-level permutation** (permute the 7×3 class→label table; 5040 perms), since
  the label is a function of 7 classes only. Report η² and the permutation rank, not a
  fabricated tiny p (the floor is 1/5040).
- Sanity: reproduce the preview table on sims 0–3 before the 100-sim run.

### Stage 4 — time-resolved copying check (agreed scope)
For t=0..3 and the lag labels `lag0 = T(t−1→t)`, `lag1 = T(t−2→t−1)`, `lag2 = T(t−3→t−2)`
(available for t≥1, ≥2, =3), compute `eta2(SV_k coord at t, lag ℓ)`. Prediction from the
copying reading of the U·V result: lag0 on SV1 at every t, lag1 on SV2 at t≥2, lag2 on SV3
at t=3 — information stepping down the SV index one step per timestep. Summarise as a
(lag × SV) matrix per t and one number: fraction of η² mass on the predicted diagonal vs the
same statistic with SV indices permuted. Caveat to print: the network is trained only on the
final step, so intermediate-step structure need not match.

### Figures (own style, 2-class palette distinct from the red/blue already used for nulls)
1. `svpc_scatter.svg` (one chosen sim, shown for two sims): SV1×SV2 coloured by *latest*
   and by *previous* (S vs D) and SV2×SV3; PC1×PC2, PC1×PC3, PC2×PC3 coloured by *first*,
   *previous*, *latest*; the 7 classes marked so the "3rd axis needed" is visible. Replaces
   the broken `plot_3D_PCA_2D_proj` colouring.
2. `svpc_overlap.svg`: mean |cos| heatmap; PC_j cumulative mass vs SV index with r/N; principal
   angles; variance along SV_k vs PC_k.
3. `svpc_decoding.svg`: η² heatmaps (SV rows | PC rows × 3 labels), winner-axis histograms.
4. `svpc_time.svg`: per-timestep lag × SV η² panels and the diagonal-mass statistic.

### Self-test (`--selftest`)
- The label table for the 7 templates equals the hand-derived one; labels determine the
  class uniquely; pairwise label correlation −1/6.
- Invariance of every reported statistic under random per-axis sign flips and under an
  orthogonal change of the *activity* coordinates applied consistently to W and X.
- Synthetic recovery: `X = a·t_latest·v1 + b·t_prev·v2 + noise` ⇒ η² of SV1/SV2 ≈ 1 for
  the right labels and ≈ 0 otherwise; PCs of that X align with v1,v2 in variance order.

## Caveats stated up front
- The preview is 4 sims; the "PC1 = first transition, latest absent from PC1–4" result is
  not yet established and may be a centring/choice artefact; the full run decides.
- PCs are centred by assumption; SVs are computed on the weights, so SV-vs-PC overlap is
  meaningful per sim (shared unit coordinates) but *not* across sims.
- Both bases are low-dimensional, so the Haar level 1/√N and r/N are weak baselines; the
  informative contrasts are within-sim (one axis vs the best of the others), as in the
  earlier rank-profile work.
- Single dataset (`outBiasTrue`, full-rank, 100 sims); no rank-constrained or other-N runs.
